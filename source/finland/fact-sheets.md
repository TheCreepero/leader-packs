## armas_eino_martola

Armas-Eino Martola (1896-1986), Finnish Army; corps commander.

- Jääkäri: fought with the Jäger battalion at Misse, the Gulf of Riga and the Aa river in 1916-17. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- First Finnish officer to earn the diploma of the French École Supérieure de Guerre (1921). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- 1925: back at the General Staff from the War College, head of its mobilisation department (liikekannallepano-osasto). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- 1927: commander of the Savo Jäger Regiment (Savon jääkärirykmentti). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- Colonel from 1931; chief of staff of the Suojeluskunta (Civil Guard) organisation 1933-1940. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- Winter War (1940): commander of the 1st Division on the Karelian Isthmus. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- Major general in 1941; commander of the 2nd Division on the Karelian Isthmus in 1942. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- 6 July to 21 September 1944: commander of VI Army Corps, which fought in the 1944 Soviet offensive on the Karelian Isthmus and later in Ladoga Karelia. Sources: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola); https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suomen_VI_armeijakunta (Suomen VI armeijakunta)
- Knight of the Mannerheim Cross (2nd class), no. 130, 1944. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)

## kaarlo_heiskanen

Kaarlo Aleksanteri Heiskanen (1894-1962), Finnish Army; division commander. Not the same man as Juho Heiskanen (FIN_juho_heiskanen).

- Jääkäri: served in the Jäger pioneer company and took the demolition course at Polangen. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Attended the first general course of the War College in 1924. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Colonel from 16 May 1938, commanding the Tampere Regiment at Hennala, Lahti. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Winter War: commander of Infantry Regiment 17 (later JR 7) of the 6th Division, defending Summa until withdrawn on 13 February 1940. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Interim peace: commander of the Central Finland Military District. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Continuation War 1941-44: commander of the 11th Division; major general in 1942. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- In 1941 his division advanced north of Lake Ladoga: actions at Jänisjärvi, Suvilahti and Tšalkki, a breakthrough on the Säämäjärvi-Sotjärvi isthmus and the capture of Prääsä, opening the road to Petroskoi, with good results and small losses. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Knight of the Mannerheim Cross no. 21 (7 October 1941); led the parade in Petroskoi. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)
- Described as calm and deliberate in command. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaarlo_Heiskanen (Kaarlo Heiskanen)

## oiva_koivisto

Oiva Koivisto (1904-1987), Finnish Navy; ship and staff officer.

- Naval cadet instructor 1927-32. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- Captain of the gunboat Karjala 1930-32; on the training ship Suomen Joutsen's cruise 1932-33. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- Commander-captain in 1936, commanding the naval company of Coastal Artillery Regiment 3 (1933-37); general staff officer 1937-39. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- Winter War, 1939-40: chief of staff of the Coastal Fleet (Rannikkolaivasto). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- 1940: chaired the Finnish delegation of the Hanko border commission; staff officer qualification in 1940. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- 1940-1944: commander of the coastal defence ship Väinämöinen. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
- 1944-45: commander of the Turku Naval Base; commodore (kommodori) in 1944. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oiva_Koivisto (Oiva Koivisto)
