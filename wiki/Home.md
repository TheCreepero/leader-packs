# Leader Packs Wiki

Historical generals, field marshals and admirals for Hearts of Iron IV that the game leaves out, one Workshop item per country (`Commanders Pack: <Country>`). Every portrait is an AI-assisted repaint of a period photograph from Wikimedia Commons; each pack's page lists the photographs with their source, author and licence.

## Packs
- [Commanders Pack: Finland](Commanders-Pack-Finland)

## About the packs
- Each pack works alone and beside any other pack.
- Each pack needs a new game [SCR-077].
- With The Road to 56, a commander that mod already gives is left out, so nobody appears twice.
