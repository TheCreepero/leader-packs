## andre_dody
Name: André Marie François Dody / Andre Dody
Lived: 1887-1960 (born Besancon 6 Oct 1887, died Paris 2 June 1960). Source: https://www.provence7.com/portails/armees/generaux-et-amiraux-de-la-bataille-de-provence-en-1944/ (Généraux et Amiraux de la Bataille de Provence en 1944)
Ids elsewhere: none (not in vanilla or Road to 56)
Suggested mode: new.
Suggested role: corps_commander. Led the 2nd Moroccan Infantry Division in the first French landing in Italy, 1943-44; lieutenant general March/May 1945.
1936-1939: March 1936 to 1 Oct 1938 commanded the 150th Infantry Regiment (so a colonel on 1 Jan 1936 in a regimental/staff post: exact post UNVERIFIED); Oct 1938-July 1939 student at the Centre des Hautes Etudes Militaires; from 7 July 1939 infantry commander of the 12th Infantry Division; brigadier general 8 Dec 1939. Source: https://generals.dk/general/Dody/Andr%C3%A9-Marie-Fran%C3%A7ois/France.html (The Generals of WWII: Dody)
Commands:
- 1 Apr 1940 to 25 Jun 1940: 8th Infantry Division (general officer commanding). Source: same page
- 1 Jul-17 Aug 1940: sub-division Bourges. Source: same page
- 17 Aug 1940 to 21 Apr 1943: territorial division and military region of Meknes, Morocco (Vichy-era post). Major general 20 Feb 1942. Source: same page
- 21 Apr-1 May 1943: March Division, Meknes. Source: same page
- 1 May 1943 to 18 Sep 1944: 2nd Moroccan Division in Italy (en-wiki gives April 1943 to September 1944; generals.dk dates adopted). Source: same page; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Moroccan_Infantry_Division (2nd Moroccan Infantry Division)
- Nov 1943 to June 1944: division in the French Expeditionary Corps under Juin; Garigliano 1 (11) May 1944. Source: https://www.provence7.com/portails/armees/generaux-et-amiraux-de-la-bataille-de-provence-en-1944/ (Généraux et Amiraux...); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Moroccan_Infantry_Division
- 1 Oct 1944 to 1 Jun 1946: Military Governor of Metz and GOC 21st Military Region; lieutenant general 25 Mar 1945 (generals.dk; provence7 says général de corps d'armée on 8 May 1945: sources differ by six weeks); inspector of infantry from 28 Dec 1945. Source: generals.dk page
Facts for stats:
- The division was the first French division to land in Italy; embarked at Bizerte Nov 1943. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2nd_Moroccan_Infantry_Division
- Fought at San Michele di Pantano (12-22 Dec 1943), Mainarde (23-29 Dec), Costa San Pietro and Monacasale (12-15 Jan 1944), Monte Croce (21 Jan 1944). The page ties them to the division, not to Dody personally; he commanded it from April/May 1943. Source: same page
- Seized Monte Majo on 11 May 1944 (Garigliano), pursued toward Rome 23 May-4 June, marched through Rome 15 June, advanced to Siena and Florence. Source: same page
- De Gaulle gave him the Croix de Guerre with palm in Rome in June 1944 (provence7, which the search summary flagged as garbled in places): UNVERIFIED beyond that page.
- Staff/training: Centre des Hautes Etudes Militaires 1938-39. Source: generals.dk page
- Formed the division in Morocco in 1943 (en-wiki: formed in Morocco from May 1943). Source: en.wikipedia 2nd Moroccan Infantry Division
- Infantry speciality (regiment, division, infantry inspector). Source: generals.dk page
