Name: Harald Öhquist / Harald Ohquist

1936-1939:
- 1 Jan 1936: lieutenant general (1935), army corps commander; the page does not say which corps. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Öhquist (Harald Öhquist)
- Nov 1939: lieutenant general, commanded II Army Corps at the outbreak of the Winter War. Source: same page.

- II Army Corps, Winter War from its outbreak, end date not given. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Öhquist (Harald Öhquist)
- Liaison officer at German HQ at the start of the Continuation War. Same source.
- Kannas Group (Kannaksen ryhmä), March 1942 to February 1944. Same source.
- IV Army Corps, winter 1944; replaced by Taavetti Laatikainen in February 1944 (the page's timeline is unclear). Same source.
- Inspector of military training, February to November 1944. Same source.

- Taught at the Markovila military school near Viipuri before 1939. Source: same page.
- Inspector of military training 1944. Source: same page.
- Page names no specific Continuation War battle. Source: same page.

- Tornio landing, Lapland War. Same source.

Not a conviction; owner decides. Commons photo likely (Mannerheim Cross holder; UNVERIFIED).
