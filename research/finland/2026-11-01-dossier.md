## einar_vihma
Name: Einar August Vihma (born Wichmann; surname changed 1936). ASCII: Einar Vihma
Lived: 1893-1944
Ids elsewhere: none
Suggested mode: new. No Vihma/Wichmann id in either set.
Suggested role: corps_commander. Led divisions (10th, 7th, 12th, 6th); no corps command.
1936-1939: 1936-1939 commander of the Suomen Valkoinen Kaarti (White Guard), colonel (colonel since 6 Dec 1933); 1933-36 director of the Cadet School. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einar_Vihma (Einar Vihma)
Commands:
- Winter War: first commander of 1st Brigade, then third commander of 10th Division (no dates given). Source: fi.wikipedia Einar Vihma
- 9 Jan 1940 to end of Winter War: 7th Division. Source: fi.wikipedia Einar Vihma
- Interim peace: Helsinki military district. Source: fi.wikipedia Einar Vihma
- Continuation War: 12th Division, then 6th Division from 18 Dec 1941 until his death 5 Aug 1944 (killed by mortar fire at Ihantala church hill with chief of staff Gösta Palkama). Major general 18 Dec 1941. Source: fi.wikipedia Einar Vihma
Facts for stats:
- Summer 1944 the 6th Division was moved from East Karelia to Ihantala and reinforced Lagus' Panzer Division, which was close to breaking; Mannerheim praised it in his memoirs. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einar_Vihma (Einar Vihma)
- Mannerheim Cross awarded 12 Oct 1941; led from the front, armed, a legend among his men. Source: same
- Harsh discipline: reported executions of deserters in 1944 (unsourced in the article); Kajaani liquor-store looting 1942 linked partly to his discipline (uncertain). Source: same (flagged unsourced there)
- Director of the Cadet School 1933-36 (training). Source: same
Appearance: Article gives no description; only a jääkäri-era photo caption. Jääkäri, officer; UNVERIFIED beyond that.
Notes: War-crimes note: Aug-Sep 1941 Saunojankangas, Soviet prisoners shot on order of a captain, Vihma approved; Supreme Court convicted the captain and treated Vihma's order as mitigating. Source: same. Wikipedia tags several claims as unsourced. Owner decides. Kansallisbiografia not checked.

## armas_eino_martola
Used: 2026-11-01-finland.md
Name: Armas-Eino Martola. ASCII: Armas-Eino Martola
Lived: 1896-1986
Ids elsewhere: none
Suggested mode: new. No Martola in either set.
Suggested role: corps_commander. Commanded 1st and 2nd Divisions and VI Army Corps in 1944.
1936-1939: colonel (since 1931); 1933-1940 chief of staff of the Suojeluskunta (Civil Guard) organisation. Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
Commands:
- Winter War (1940): 1st Division, Karelian Isthmus. Source: fi.wikipedia Armas-Eino Martola
- Continuation War 1942: 2nd Division, Karelian Isthmus. Major general 1941. Source: same
- July 1944: VI Army Corps, Tali-Ihantala, later Ladoga Karelia. Source: same
Facts for stats:
- Commanded VI Corps in the Tali-Ihantala battle (Kannas great offensive). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)
- First Finnish officer to earn the diploma of the French École Supérieure de Guerre (1921). Source: same
- Mannerheim Cross (2nd class) 1944, knight no. 130. Source: same
- Civil Guard chief of staff 1933-40 (mobilisation, reserve organisation). Source: same
- Jääkäri: fought at Misse, Riga Gulf, Aa river 1916-17. Source: same
Appearance: No description; wartime photos exist. UNVERIFIED.
Notes: Dates of Winter War division command are given only as 1940. Post-war: foreign minister 1944, UNFICYP commander 1966-69 (outside the period).
Correction, 2026-11-01 (planner, after the plan review): the Martola article does not name Tali-Ihantala. It says only that he took command of VI Army Corps in July 1944, "joka taisteli Kannaksen suurhyökkäyksessä ja myöhemmin Laatokan Karjalassa". Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola). The corps' list of commanders gives "jääkärikenraalimajuri Ilmari Martola 6. heinäkuuta – 21. syyskuuta 1944". Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suomen_VI_armeijakunta (Suomen VI armeijakunta). The Tali-Ihantala article (25 June to 9 July 1944) lists the Finnish IV Corps there, not VI Corps (reviewer's reading, https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talin–Ihantalan_taistelu). The two "Tali-Ihantala" lines above are therefore unsupported.
Added facts, 2026-11-01, from the same Martola article: "Sotakorkeakoulusta hän palasi Yleisesikuntaan 1925 ja sijoitettiin liikekannallepano-osaston päälliköksi" (head of the General Staff's mobilisation department, 1925); "Vuonna 1927 Martola nimitettiin Savon jääkärirykmentin komentajaksi" (commander of the Savo Jäger Regiment, 1927). Source: https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armas-Eino_Martola (Armas-Eino Martola)

Facts for stats:
- Division advanced north of Ladoga: Jänisjärvi actions, Suvilahti, Tšalkki, breakthrough on Säämäjärvi-Sotjärvi isthmus, capture of Prääsä, opened road to Petroskoi; good results with small losses. Source: same
- Mannerheim Cross no. 21 (7 Oct 1941); led the Petroskoi parade. Source: same
- Calm, deliberate style; attended first War College general course 1924; jääkäri pioneer company, Polangen demolition course. Source: same
- Post-war Defence Forces commander 1953-59 (outside period).
Appearance: No description; character quiet and calm. UNVERIFIED.
Notes: Post-war suspect in the weapons-cache affair (charges dropped 1948). Rank dates: lieutenant general 1951.
