# Dossier: Poland (POL), pack poland, 2026-11-06

Method: pages fetched with WebFetch (English Wikipedia, dzieje.pl, pl.wikipedia, timenote.info mirror of Polish Wikipedia, polishexilesofww2.org). Several IPN PDFs could not be read (compressed streams) and are not cited. About 22 web calls used.
Snapshot check: none of the persons below appear (under any spelling checked) as a unit leader or advisor in either snapshot set. Checked: Rowecki, Szylling, Chruściel, Duch, Okulicki, Dindorf-Ankowicz, Olszyna-Wilczyński, Berling, Plisowski, Świerczewski, Odzierzyński. All are suggested mode `new`. Józef Zając (POL_jozef_zajac, vanilla advisor) was looked at and left out: air force chief and deputy corps commander, not a clear field commander.
Photo licences were NOT checked (another agent does that). Persons who died before 1970 (Rowecki, Okulicki, Chruściel, Olszyna-Wilczyński, Plisowski) and Polish state-agency 1939-45 portraits are the likeliest public domain; Duch, Szylling, Dindorf-Ankowicz lived to 1963-1980 (photos from the war years usually still fine).
Ranked order of preference for 4 slots: szylling, rowecki, chrusciel, duch; reserves: okulicki, dindorf_ankowicz, olszyna_wilczynski, berling; further: plisowski, swierczewski, odzierzynski.

## antoni_szylling
Name: Antoni Szylling (ASCII: Antoni Szylling)
Lived: 1884-1971
Ids elsewhere: none
Suggested mode: new (not in either snapshot set)
Suggested role: field_marshal (commanded Army Kraków in 1939; an army commander). corps_commander is the safer alternative if the planner wants no field marshals from Poland.
1936-1939: Brigadier general since 1929; commanded the 23rd Division in Katowice and the 8th Division in Modlin (dates UNVERIFIED); in May 1937 worked at the General Inspectorate of the Armed Forces in Silesia; 1 Jan 1936 post UNVERIFIED. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Szylling (Antoni Szylling). In March 1939 he was made commander of Army Kraków (formed 23 March 1939, per IPN search snippet only; the Wikipedia page says "In 1939 he commanded Army Kraków" with no date).
Commands:
- 1939 (formed March, fought from 2 Sep to 20 Sep): Army Kraków (6th, 7th, 23rd, 55th Inf Div, 21st and 22nd Mountain Div, a mountain brigade, 2 cavalry brigades), southern front; attacked from 2 Sep by German 14th Army (List); withdrew, avoided encirclement, reached Lublin area; 19 Sep joined Army Lublin (Piskor) at Tomaszów Lubelski; encircled and surrendered 20 Sep. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Szylling (Antoni Szylling)
- 1939-30 Apr 1945: prisoner, Oflag VII-A Murnau; promoted generał dywizji in 1946. Source: same page.
Facts for stats:
- Called with Thommée and Maczek "one of the most successful Polish commanders in the 1939 invasion". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Szylling (Antoni Szylling)
- Army Kraków was "the main pivot of Polish defence", tasked to delay the German advance and defend Upper Silesia. Source: same.
- Avoided several German attempts to encircle him in a fighting withdrawal to the Lublin area. Source: same.
- Commanded 44th Infantry Regiment in the Polish-Soviet War; then divisions 28th (Warsaw), 23rd (Katowice), 8th (Modlin). Source: same.
- Joined the Polish 2nd Corps in the East in 1917; PPS Combat Organization member in youth. Source: same.
Appearance: no description on the page; interwar general's uniform with rogatywka likely (UNVERIFIED).
Notes: Wikipedia states the page does not give his rank in Aug-Sep 1939 (brigadier general since 1929, so generał brygady). Decorations: Gold Cross of Virtuti Militari, Commander's Cross of Polonia Restituta, Cross of Valour x3, Gold Cross of Merit. Died in Montreal.

## stefan_rowecki
Used: 2026-11-06-poland.md
Name: Stefan Paweł Rowecki "Grot" (ASCII: Stefan Pawel Rowecki)
Lived: 1895-1944
Ids elsewhere: none
Suggested mode: new
Suggested role: field_marshal (commander-in-chief of ZWZ/Home Army) or corps_commander for the 1939 brigade; planner's choice.
1936-1939: Early 1936 commanded the KOP brigade "Podole"; 1938 deputy commander (infantry) of the 2nd Legions Infantry Division in Kielce; June 1939 formed the Warsaw Armoured-Motorized Brigade. Source: https://dzieje.pl/node/106781 (Stefan Rowecki. Fot. PAP/CAF/Archiwum). On 1 Jan 1936 he may still have been 55th Regiment commander (1930-1935 per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Rowecki); rank colonel (płk dypl.) in 1939 per https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warszawska_Brygada_Pancerno-Motorowa (Warszawska Brygada Pancerno-Motorowa).
Commands:
- 10 Jun-20 Sep 1939: Warsaw Armoured-Motorized Brigade (formed 10 Jun, name set 19 Jun). 1 Sep concentrated near Garwolin; Army Lublin; 13 Sep attack toward Annopol succeeded, then retreat toward Lwów; 16-17 Sep encircled near Tomaszów Lubelski; fuel scrapped; last fight the battle of Tomaszów Lubelski 17-20 Sep; brigade dissolved 20 Sep. Source: https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warszawska_Brygada_Pancerno-Motorowa
- Oct 1939: chief of staff and deputy commander of SZP; Jan 1940 ZWZ Warsaw area; 30 Jun 1940 ZWZ commander-in-chief; 14 Feb 1942 commander of the Home Army (AK). Source: https://dzieje.pl/node/106781
- 30 Jun 1943 arrested by the Gestapo; murdered Sachsenhausen between 2 and 7 Aug 1944 (en.wikipedia says 2 Aug 1944). Source: same.
Facts for stats:
- 1941 organised sabotage operation "Wachlarz" in territory east of pre-war borders. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Rowecki (Stefan Rowecki)
- Commanded 55th Infantry Regiment in Leszno 1930-1935. Source: same.
- Staff-college graduate (płk dypl.); lieutenant colonel dyplomowany 1926. Sources: pl.wikipedia brigade page; dzieje.pl/node/106781.
- Brigade used 7TP and TKS tanks; did not reach full mobilisation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Rowecki
- Refused a German offer of anti-Bolshevik cooperation. Source: https://dzieje.pl/node/106781
Appearance: no description. A 1915 photo exists on Polish Wikipedia/Commons (Plik:Stefan Rowecki - 1915.jpg) and an early-1930s photo on en.wikipedia (UNVERIFIED which licence). Probably rogatywka, general uniform.
Notes: Rank disagreement: en.wikipedia infobox "major general"; dzieje.pl says generał brygady from May 1940. Decorations include Virtuti Militari Gold Cross (1942), Order of White Eagle (posth. 1995). Awarded Légion d'honneur 1937. No war crimes; en.wikipedia says he authorised aid to the Warsaw Ghetto underground.

Suggested mode: new
Suggested role: corps_commander (led the Warsaw Home Army Corps in 1944; a regimental commander in 1939).
1936-1939: Jan 1937 deputy commander of the 40th Infantry Regiment; March 1938 commanding officer of the 82nd Siberian Infantry Regiment, Brest-Litovsk (30th Inf Div). 1 Jan 1936 post UNVERIFIED. Rank in 1939 not stated on page (colonel before Sept 1944 promotion). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Chru%C5%9Bciel (Antoni Chruściel)
Commands:
- Mar-Sep 1939: 82nd Infantry Regiment, mobilised 23-27 March; in combat from 2 Sep in Piotrków Operational Group, Łódź Army; then defence of Modlin Fortress until capitulation. Source: same.
- Jun-Oct 1940: ZWZ staff 3rd division; from Oct 1940 deputy commander of the Warsaw ZWZ region; Home Army chief of staff. Source: same.
- 1 Aug-Sep 1944: commander of the Warsaw District, de facto commander of all insurgents in the Warsaw Uprising; 20 Sep reorganised into the Warsaw Home Army Corps (Śródmieście, Żoliborz, Mokotów divisions). Promoted general 14 Sep 1944. Source: same.
- Captive Stalag XIII-D, Oflag IV-C Colditz; liberated May 1945; later Polish II Corps in Italy (until 1948). Source: same.
Facts for stats:
- Regiment held Modlin Fortress in 1939. Source: same.
- Ordered full Home Army mobilisation for 17:00 on 1 Aug 1944 with Bór-Komorowski. Source: same.
- Described as de facto commander of the whole uprising. Source: same.
- Virtuti Militari Gold Cross (28 Sep 1939) and Silver (1921); Cross of Valour x4; Austro-Hungarian Karl Troop Cross, Bravery Medal x2. Source: same.
Appearance: no description; infobox photo "Chruściel in the United Kingdom". A well-known Commons photo "Warsaw Uprising Antoni Chruściel (1944).jpg" exists (found in search list only; licence UNVERIFIED). Typical 1944: field cap/rogatywka, AK armband.
Notes: Brigadier general only from 14 Sep 1944; colonel in 1939. Commanded a regiment in 1939, so corps role is an upgrade based on 1944. Bór-Komorowski (POL_Tadeusz_Komorowski, RT56 advisor) is a different person.
